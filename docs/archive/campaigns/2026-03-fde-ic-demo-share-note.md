# Forward Deployed Engineer (IC) Demo — Share Note (March 2026 Campaign Snapshot)

## Share Note

Below is a short note that can be pasted into email or Slack.

---

I ran two sourcing cycles against the IC Forward Deployed Engineer brief to test whether the agent could still surface credible talent in what we were treating as a tapped market.

Across the two runs, the system evaluated **801 candidates**, escalated **126** to deeper review, and surfaced **50 named saves** across the two run summaries. The most important takeaway is that the strongest results did **not** come from obvious title-based searches. The best-performing Booleans consistently targeted:

- backend infrastructure engineers who have pivoted into GenAI
- document-understanding / production-GenAI builders
- founding or 0-to-1 engineers
- consulting / field engineers who have shipped GenAI into enterprise environments

The facial screen remained tight in both runs at roughly **17%**, which is what we would want for a role with a narrow technical + delivery intersection. Once the agent escalated a profile to deeper review, conversion stayed strong, which suggests the screening logic was doing real filtering rather than keyword matching.

The richest candidate pools were:

- consulting / field engineers with hands-on GenAI delivery
- backend engineers with production systems depth plus recent LLM / agentic work
- fintech / financial-services builders
- founding engineers at AI startups
- big-tech or hyperscaler engineers with platform or customer-delivery experience

The strongest recurring candidate signals were **RAG**, **agentic workflows**, **backend / distributed systems grounding**, and clear production language such as **built**, **shipped**, **deployed**, or **architected**.

The two biggest learnings for future runs are:

1. Lead with **backend-framework × GenAI** and **document-understanding × production** variants rather than title-led searches.
2. Expand the **consulting / field-engineer** vein, which looks materially richer than expected for this role.
