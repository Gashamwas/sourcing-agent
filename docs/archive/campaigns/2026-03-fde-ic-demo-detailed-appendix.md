## Run A

### Executive Summary

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Strings executed | 20 of 37 |
| Strings skipped | 17 |
| Total search results | 44,732 |
| Pages reviewed | 65 |
| Candidates evaluated | 367 |
| Facial YES | 59 |
| Facial NO | 307 |
| Saves | 34 |
| Rejected after full review | 24 |
| Facial YES rate | 16.8% |
| Reported full-review save rate | 47.8% |
| Overall save rate | 9.3% |

**Interpretation:** This was a productive run in what was explicitly flagged as a tapped market. The facial screen stayed tight at 16.8%, and the full-review save rate remained strong. The most effective pattern was not a title-led search; it was a capability-led search for backend engineers, production builders, consulting ICs, and founding engineers who had layered GenAI delivery onto real systems work.

### Top Performing Strings

| String | Why It Mattered | Reported Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **#4 — Backend Frameworks × GenAI** | Operationalized the insight that the best FDE candidates are backend engineers who pivoted into GenAI rather than ML researchers who drifted into applications | 17 saves from 6 pages; highest absolute save count |
| **#2 — Document Understanding / Processing × GenAI** | Targeted a canonical enterprise deployment use case and surfaced engineers who had actually built extraction / understanding pipelines with LLMs | 10 saves from 5 pages; highest reported precision string |
| **#63 — Fintech × Founding / Staff × LLM** | Adaptive spawn from earlier signal; proved that a tiny, refined fintech pool could still be highly productive | 7 saves from 4 pages |
| **#37 — 0-to-1 Builder Archetype** | Used self-descriptive builder language such as “built from scratch” and “0-to-1” rather than pure technology keywords | 6 saves from 4 pages |
| **#19 — Founding Engineers at AI Startups** | Small but highly concentrated pool of end-to-end builders | 5 saves from 2 pages |
| **#65 — Enterprise GenAI Integration Pattern** | Targeted engineers integrating GenAI into existing enterprise systems, which maps directly to the role | 5 saves from 6 pages |

### Dominant Candidate Archetypes

| Archetype | Description |
|---|---|
| Backend Engineer → GenAI Pivot | Strong systems or distributed-systems grounding, then recent move into LLM / agentic delivery |
| Consulting / Field Engineer with GenAI Delivery | Engineers at consulting firms or vendor field teams who personally built and shipped GenAI for enterprise clients |
| Founding / Early Engineer at AI Startup | 0-to-1 builders who owned full-stack delivery in ambiguous environments |
| Big Tech AI Platform Builder | Engineers at FAANG / hyperscalers building internal GenAI platforms or products |
| Enterprise-Internal GenAI Builder | Engineers at large enterprises building internal AI systems; strong technically, but often lighter on multi-customer delivery evidence |

### Noise Patterns

The dominant noise came from:

- ML / data scientists without production engineering depth
- engineering managers or directors who had stopped building
- product managers and strategy leaders with AI vocabulary but no authorship
- DevOps / MLOps profiles that operated platforms without building the application layer
- pre-sales or solutions profiles without clear production build evidence
- academic or PhD-heavy research profiles
- classical ML engineers who had not made the GenAI pivot

### Adaptation Notes

The most productive adaptation chain came off **String #15 (Consulting × AI)**. Its spawn family generated a large share of the run’s most compelling candidates, especially around:

- fintech × founding / staff × LLM builders
- consulting leaders × GenAI delivery
- large-enterprise AI platform builders
- labelbox / forward-deployed and adjacent field-builder profiles

### Underperformers and Mechanical Truncation

| String | Issue |
|---|---|
| **#27 — CTOs / VPs at small startups** | Far too broad, title-led, and structurally misaligned with an IC builder role |
| **#16 — RAG + production evidence** | Likely a sound concept that was cut short by timeout issues |
| **#62 — Google Field Solutions Architect** | Over-specified micro-pool with too little room for narrowing |
| **#12 — Customer-facing engineers at AI companies** | Concept validated by at least one save but truncated before enough pages were reviewed |
| **#59 — Consulting × seniority × AI** | Produced one exceptional candidate but lower overall yield than sharper consulting variants |

### Recommendations from Run A

The run suggests doubling down on:

- consulting / professional services engineers with real GenAI delivery evidence
- backend-framework × GenAI strings
- fintech / financial-services GenAI builders
- healthcare / pharma delivery builders
- customer / client / engagement qualifiers
- delivery-oriented language such as `built for`, `deployed for`, `delivered to`

5. Keep expanding the **consulting-IC builder** pool, especially at Tier 2 consulting and services firms.

## Shared Takeaways Across Both Runs

### What worked repeatedly

- Backend-framework signals were the highest-yield Boolean family.
- Document-understanding / production-GenAI strings were consistently strong.
- Founding engineers and 0-to-1 builders remained a high-fit pool.
- Consulting and field engineers shipping GenAI into enterprise environments were one of the richest underexplored veins.
- Financial-services and fintech builders repeatedly surfaced strong candidates.

### What did not work

- Title-first executive searches without technical qualifiers
- Prompt-engineering-led strings
- Canonical exact-title / exact-company FDE searches in a tapped market
- Hyper-narrow sniper strings on pools below roughly 30–50 results

### Practical recruiting takeaway

The best IC Forward Deployed Engineer candidates do **not** primarily present as “forward deployed engineers” on LinkedIn. They are more often:

- backend or platform engineers who now ship GenAI systems
- consulting or field engineers who build directly for enterprise clients
- founding or early engineers who have shipped end-to-end production AI
- staff-level enterprise builders with recent agentic / RAG / orchestration depth

That is the core sourcing insight these runs validated.
